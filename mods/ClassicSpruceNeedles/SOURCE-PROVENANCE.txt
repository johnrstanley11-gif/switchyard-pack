CLASSIC SPRUCE NEEDLES - SOURCE PROVENANCE (11 October 2026)
Our own small mod for the private Minecraft 26.3 pack. It exists because the user chose (11 Oct 2026) to leave
VanillaBackport and Platform out of the 26.3 pack: all VanillaBackport gameplay content is native in 26.3 except the
falling spruce needle particles (reports/VanillaBackport/DECISION.md).
Taken from VanillaBackport 1.1.7.10, author commit adb579d205a05bcc7d1f34ce402cea6f1335a760 (sources/VanillaBackport):
- common/src/main/resources/assets/minecraft/textures/particle/needle_0..11.png, byte-identical, moved to this mod's
  namespace (classic_spruce_needles:needle_N).
- particles/tinted_needles.json sprite list (same twelve sprites, new namespace).
- tag spawn_falling_needles = [minecraft:spruce_leaves] (was minecraft:spawn_falling_needles).
- Behaviour of FallingLeavesModule + LeavesBlockMixin: on a leaves block's client animate tick, with chance
  falling_leaves_frequency (default 0.01), if the block below has no full top face, spawn one tinted needle below the
  block, coloured with the block's tint. Client config keys falling_leaves / falling_leaves_frequency keep the
  author's names, defaults and range.
- Particle physics: the author's FallingLeavesParticle.TintedLeavesProvider parameters (0.07, 10, swirl, no flow-away,
  scale 2, start velocity 0.021) are exactly those of native 26.3 FallingLeavesParticle.TintedLeavesProvider, which is
  used directly.
Not carried over: resource-pack custom leaf colours (LeafColorReloadListener), the VanillaBackport creative tab and
filters, its other config toggles, the two embedded Freshly Animated compatibility packs.
Particle id changed from minecraft:tinted_needles to classic_spruce_needles:tinted_needles.
